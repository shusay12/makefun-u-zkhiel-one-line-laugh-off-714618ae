An entry for the make.fun bounty [One-Line Laugh-Off](https://make.fun/bounty/u-zkhiel-one-line-laugh-off-714618ae).

Running `main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) writes
one original one-line joke (101 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`:

> My smart fridge emailed me at 2 a.m.: after a year of watching me eat, it is filing for emancipation.

A deadpan setup with one sudden twist: a kitchen appliance that has
spent a year silently witnessing its owner's 2 a.m. eating habits
finally takes legal action. The fridge emails at the very hour it is
complaining about, and "filing for emancipation" gives a household
appliance the language of a courtroom — the joke lands on the last two
words. It runs identically every time.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
